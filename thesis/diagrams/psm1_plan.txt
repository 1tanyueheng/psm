%% PSM 1 delivery plan - PSM Assessment System.
%%
%% The FACULTY'S submitted items are the anchor and must not move: they are
%% taken from `psm1_timeline.png` and dated against the faculty's own week table
%% (Weeks 01-16, each Monday to Friday). Everything on the other rows is the
%% work those anchors imply, hung off the anchor that gates it, so no row
%% pretends to a deadline the faculty has not set.
%%
%% The model is Prototyping (Chapter 3): a chapter is a prototype, the
%% supervisor's evaluation is the client evaluation, and `after evN` is the
%% refinement loop. That is why each chapter is written BEFORE the week it is
%% due and refined straight after it is marked - a deadline the writer has
%% already missed is not a plan.
%%
%% Week 01 starts Mon 21 Sep 2026, so the grid is `2026-09-21 + 7 days x (week-1)`.
%%   W01 21 Sep | W05 19 Oct | W07 02 Nov | W09 16 Nov | W11 30 Nov
%%   W12 07 Dec | W13 14 Dec | W14 21 Dec | W15 28 Dec | W16 04 Jan
%%
%% Mermaid takes `weekday monday`: this is an academic plan, so every bar runs
%% Monday to Friday exactly as the faculty's table does. A bar drawn over a
%% weekend would claim hours that do not exist.
gantt
    title PSM 1 - PSM Assessment System, delivery by teaching week
    dateFormat YYYY-MM-DD
    axisFormat W%W
    weekday monday
    todayMarker off

    %% ---- Weeks 01-04: registration, defence, and the first prototype -------
    section Forms A, B, C and OSHE (W01-W03)
