sequenceDiagram
    autonumber
    participant S as Student /<br/>Supervisor
    participant UI as Registration UI<br/>(React SPA)
    participant C as Registration<br/>Controller
    participant SVC as Registration<br/>Service
    participant A as Agreement +<br/>Supervision
    participant B as Lampiran B +<br/>Milestone

    S->>UI: Fill in Lampiran A and submit
    UI->>C: POST /api/registrations/agreements
    C->>SVC: submitAgreement(...)
    SVC->>SVC: Validate term open, no project,<br/>title usable, supervisor serves<br/>the programme
    SVC->>A: Save the agreement<br/>status = pending_supervisor
    C-->>UI: 201 Created

    S->>UI: Choose one proposed title<br/>and acknowledge
    UI->>C: POST .../{agreement}/acknowledge
    C->>SVC: acknowledgeBySupervisor(...)
    SVC->>A: Create the supervision and<br/>save the agreed title
    C-->>UI: Lampiran B may be filed

    S->>UI: File Lampiran B, then sign it
    UI->>SVC: PUT .../{form}/file<br/>POST .../{form}/sign
    SVC->>B: Endorse the form and<br/>open the proposal milestone
    UI-->>S: The proposal is open

    S->>UI: Withdraw the filing
    UI->>C: POST .../{agreement}/withdraw
    C->>SVC: withdrawAgreement()
    C-->>UI: 200 OK (cancelled, pairing retired)
