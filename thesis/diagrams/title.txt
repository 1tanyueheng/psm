sequenceDiagram
    autonumber
    actor S as Student /<br/>Supervisor
    participant UI as Registration UI<br/>(React SPA)
    participant C as Registration<br/>Controller
    participant SVC as Registration<br/>Service
    participant A as Agreement +<br/>Supervision
    participant B as Lampiran B +<br/>Milestone

    S->>UI: Fill in Lampiran A and submit
    UI->>C: POST /api/registrations/agreements
    C->>SVC: submitAgreement(...)
    SVC->>SVC: Confirm a term is open and no project exists
    SVC->>SVC: Confirm the supervisor serves the programme

    alt A.1 No term, or a project or agreement already exists
        C-->>UI: 422 refused, naming the cause
    else A.2 A proposed title is unusable
        C-->>UI: 422 refused, the student corrects it
    else A.3 Supervisor does not serve the programme
        C-->>UI: 422 refused
    else Valid
        SVC->>A: Save the agreement, status = pending_supervisor
        C-->>UI: 201 Created
    end

    S->>UI: Choose one proposed title and acknowledge
    UI->>C: POST .../{agreement}/acknowledge
    C->>SVC: acknowledgeBySupervisor(...)

    alt A.4 The title named is not one the student proposed
        C-->>UI: 422 refused, Part C is a choice between the candidates
    else Title is one of the three proposed
        SVC->>A: Create the supervision, record the agreed title
        C-->>UI: Lampiran B may be filed
    end

    S->>UI: File Lampiran B, then sign it
    UI->>SVC: PUT .../{form}/file and POST .../{form}/sign

    alt A.5 The supervisor sends the form back
        SVC->>B: Return the form as a draft, with a reason
        B-->>UI: Draft, waiting for the student
    else Signed
        SVC->>B: Endorse the form
        SVC->>B: Open the proposal milestone
        UI-->>S: The proposal is open
    end

    Note over S,UI: A.6 Withdrawal is a separate action, available<br/>only until a project is registered from the filing
