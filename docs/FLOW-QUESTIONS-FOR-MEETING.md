# Flow Questions for the PSM Coordinator Meeting

**Prepared for:** TAN YUE HENG
**Coordinator:** Norfaradilla Binti Wahid (FSKTM PSM Coordinator)
**Purpose:** one question per slide, for an online meeting

---

## How to use this

Each question below is written to stand alone as **one slide**. The slide wants:

- **A title** — the question itself, phrased as a question
- **Two or three bullets** — what the system currently assumes
- **One line at the bottom** — what you need from the coordinator

The "what the system assumes" bullets are the point. Showing the assumption lets
the coordinator correct it, which is far more useful than asking an open
question and getting a vague answer.

**Suggested order:** Flow questions first (this document), then the weighting
question from `COORDINATOR-MEETING-QUESTIONS.md` (2.3), because the flow decides
where the weighting question even applies.

---

# Part 1 — The end-to-end flow

## Slide 1 — Where does the process start?

**The system currently assumes:**

- A student registers a title through **Lampiran A** (supervisor agreement),
  then **Lampiran B** (title proposal), before any project exists
- The supervisor acknowledges Lampiran A; the panel decides the title at the
  **proposal milestone**
- A project record is only created once Lampiran B is submitted

**What I need:** is that the real starting order, and who may initiate each step?

---

## Slide 2 — What has to happen before a student can begin work?

**The system currently assumes:**

- The student needs an **approved title** and a **seated panel** before the
  proposal milestone can be decided
- Until then, the milestone chain does not open

**What I need:** what are the real prerequisites — and is a panel always seated,
or only when a proposal is contested?

---

## Slide 3 — Does PSM 1 always follow PSM 2, on the same title?

**The system currently assumes:**

- PSM 1 and PSM 2 are **one project across two consecutive terms**, on one title
- PSM 1 delivers the **proposal and Chapters 1–4**
- PSM 2 delivers **Chapters 5–7** and assembles the whole document
- **Chapter 4 belongs to PSM 1 only** — PSM 2 examines what was built rather
  than rebuilding it
- A rollover step carries the title forward, so the student does not re-register

**What I need:** is that correct? In particular, is Chapter 4 ever resubmitted
in PSM 2?

> **This is worth asking carefully.** An earlier version of the system had
> Chapter 4 in *both* chains, so a student running PSM 1 → PSM 2 was asked to
> submit the same chapter twice. It was removed on the reasoning above, but that
> reasoning is mine, not the faculty's.

---

## Slide 4 — Can a student take PSM 1 and PSM 2 at the same time?

**The system currently assumes:**

- Yes — both batches can run **concurrently in one term**
- They have **separate capacity ceilings** (a supervisor may be full for PSM 1
  and free for PSM 2)
- They have **separate assessment windows** the coordinator opens independently

**What I need:** does that happen in practice, and how often? It changes whether
the capacity rules should be per batch or combined.

---

## Slide 5 — What is the milestone chain, and is it fixed?

**The system currently assumes this chain, with these weights:**

| PSM 1 | Weight | PSM 2 | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proposal | 10% | Chapter 5 | 30% |
| Chapter 1 | 15% | Chapter 6 | 20% |
| Chapter 2 | 15% | Chapter 7 | 20% |
| Chapter 3 | 15% | Final Report | 30% |
| Chapter 4 | 20% | | |
| Final Report | 25% | | |

**What I need:** are these the real deliverable names, weights and order — and
may a coordinator change them per project, or are they fixed faculty-wide?

---

## Slide 6 — What are the deadlines based on?

**The system currently assumes:**

- Each milestone has an **offset in days** from the project start
  (Proposal +21, Chapter 1 +42, and so on)
- A coordinator may **override** an individual deadline, with a recorded reason
- There is a **late window** (default 7 days) after which submission is refused

**What I need:** are deadlines relative to a start date, or fixed calendar
dates set by the faculty each semester?

---

## Slide 7 — What happens when a deadline is missed?

**The system currently assumes:**

- A submission inside the late window is accepted and **flagged as late**
- After the window, submission is **refused** and the student must ask the
  coordinator for an extension
- A late assessment affects the assessor's on-time rate, not the student's mark

**What I need:** is there a **penalty**, and if so who applies it? The system
records lateness but deliberately does not deduct marks.

---

## Slide 8 — How does a milestone get approved?

**The system currently assumes this cycle:**

`Pending → Open → Submitted → Reviewed → Approved`

with two exits:

- **Rejected** — sent back, the student resubmits and the revision is numbered
- **Overdue** — the deadline passed without submission

- The **supervisor** reviews; the student may withdraw a file only while the
  milestone is still submitted

**What I need:** who approves a chapter — the supervisor alone, or does a
coordinator countersign?

---

## Slide 9 — Is the proposal milestone special?

**The system currently assumes:**

- The **proposal is decided by the panel**, not the supervisor
- It is the only milestone whose verdict is the panel's
- It has three outcomes: **approved**, **conditional approval**, **rejected**
- A conditional approval requires **Lampiran C** (amendment form) before the
  chain opens
- A rejection lets the student **change the title** and resubmit

**What I need:** is that the real process, and does a conditional approval
always need Lampiran C?

---

## Slide 10 — How does the panel decide, and does one member suffice?

**The system currently assumes:**

- A panel of **two examiners** per student (a chair and a member)
- The panel records **one decision for the whole panel**, not one each
- The panel must not include the student's own supervisor

**What I need:** is two the right size, and what happens if the panel disagrees
— does the chair decide?

---

## Slide 11 — When does marking open, and who opens it?

**The system currently assumes:**

- Marking is **closed until a coordinator opens an assessment window**
- A window is scoped to **(term, batch)** — PSM 1 and PSM 2 open separately
- While a window is shut, assessors cannot see or file draft marks
- Filed marks remain readable after the window closes

**What I need:** is that the real control, and who has authority to open and
close it — the coordinator, or the faculty?

---

## Slide 12 — What does each assessor mark?

**The system currently assumes these five forms:**

| Form | Assessed by | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Lampiran E | Supervisor, PSM 1 | 35 |
| Lampiran I | Examiner, Seminar 1 | 30 |
| Lampiran G | Supervisor, PSM 2 | 50 |
| Lampiran H | Supervisor, progress report | 5 |
| Lampiran J | Examiner, final seminar | 40 |

**What I need:** confirmation that these are the current forms and that each
assessor marks only their own.

---

## Slide 13 — When does a student see their mark?

**The system currently assumes:**

- A mark is released **automatically** once every assessor has filed
- No separate coordinator "release" step is required
- The student sees the mark but the system **does not derive a letter grade**

**What I need:** is automatic release correct, or should a coordinator or exam
board approve first?

> Related to question 2.3 in the other document: the system refuses to band a
> grade because its share is only 65% of PSM 1. If the faculty expects a letter
> grade from this system, that changes.

---

## Slide 14 — What closes a semester?

**The system currently assumes:**

- A term **cannot be closed** while marking is incomplete
- Closing archives completed projects and preserves their files
- Completed PSM 1 students **roll over** into PSM 2 on the same title

**What I need:** is "marking complete" the right gate, and is archiving at term
close the right moment?

---

# Part 2 — Roles and permissions

## Slide 15 — Who can do what?

**The system currently assumes four roles:**

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Student** | Register a title, submit milestones, see released marks |
| **Supervisor** | Review and approve milestones, mark their own students |
| **Coordinator** | Allocate supervisors and panels, open marking, close terms |
| **Admin** | Manage accounts and system settings |

- A supervisor and an examiner are the **same role** — being on a panel is a
  seating, not a separate account
- The coordinator **moderates but does not award marks**

**What I need:** is that right, and is there a role the system is missing — for
example a **lecturer** who marks the PSM 1 assignment?

---

## Slide 16 — Does a coordinator need to see everything?

**The system currently assumes:**

- A coordinator sees the **whole faculty** unless given an explicit scope
- Scopes can be limited to a **batch** or a **programme**

**What I need:** should a coordinator be limited to their own programme by
default, or see everything?

---

# Part 3 — Exceptions and edge cases

## Slide 17 — What happens when a supervisor leaves or is replaced?

**The system currently assumes:**

- An allocation can be **ended** and a new supervisor assigned
- The old supervision is closed rather than deleted, so history survives
- The student keeps their title and existing milestones

**What I need:** is that the real process, and does the panel change too?

---

## Slide 18 — What happens when a student changes their title?

**The system currently assumes:**

- A title may only change while the proposal is **rejected or conditionally
  approved**
- Once the proposal is approved the title is locked
- A change reopens the proposal for a fresh panel decision

**What I need:** is a title change ever allowed after approval, and who
authorises it?

---

## Slide 19 — What happens to a student who does not submit?

**The system currently assumes:**

- A milestone that passes its deadline becomes **overdue**
- The supervisor may issue **Lampiran D** — a warning letter
- A student with no submission gets **no supervisor mark at all**, rather than
  a zero

**What I need:** what is the real consequence of non-submission, and at what
point does a student fail or withdraw?

---

## Slide 20 — What about students who repeat or defer?

**The system currently assumes:**

- A student may hold PSM 1 in one term and PSM 2 in a later one
- A project belongs to exactly **one term**
- A repeat means a **new project** in a new term, not an edit of the old one

**What I need:** how is a repeat or deferral handled today, and should the
system carry the old work forward?

---

# Part 4 — Documents and the wider system

## Slide 21 — Which documents must the system hold?

**The system currently assumes:**

- It stores **milestone submissions** (the chapter files) and keeps every
  revision, so a superseded file is never lost
- Every download is **logged** — who read which file, and when
- Files are stored privately and served only through an authorised route

**What I need:** is there a document the system should hold that it does not —
and is there a **retention period** the faculty must meet?

---

## Slide 22 — How does this relate to Author, AITCS and eReport?

**The Sharepoint says:**

> "the rest of the forms and PSM documents submission will go into
> (i) Author system, (ii) AITCS system, (iii) eReport system"

**The system currently assumes:**

- It manages the **workflow around** the documents — allocation, milestones,
  marking windows, panels — rather than being the final repository
- Nothing is currently sent to Author, AITCS or eReport

**What I need:** is that the right boundary, or is an export or hand-off
expected?

> This is the single most important scope question. If Author already holds
> every form and document, the value here is the workflow, and that is the case
> to make.

---

## Slide 23 — Does the system need to produce anything for the faculty?

**The system currently assumes:**

- It can export reports as CSV: supervisor workload, panel workload, cohort
  progress
- It keeps an **audit log** of who did what, retained for 7 years

**What I need:** is there a report the faculty must produce each semester —
accreditation, pass rates, workload — that the system should match?

---

# Closing

## Slide 24 — What is the one thing that would help most?

**The system currently assumes** that the biggest friction is in allocation,
capacity tracking and knowing which student is where.

**What I need:** the coordinator's own answer to what is most painful today.
Whatever they say is worth more than every assumption above.

**Then ask:** would the faculty trial this with one batch next semester, and
what approvals would that need?

---

## Notes for building the slides

- **One question per slide.** Do not combine — an online meeting loses the
  thread otherwise.
- **Lead with the assumption, not the question.** "The system assumes X — is
  that right?" gets a concrete answer; "how does X work?" often does not.
- **Mark the three that matter most** (Slides 3, 12, 22) so you can protect
  them if time runs short.
- **Keep a running note of every "usually" and "sometimes"** — those are the
  exceptions the system currently treats as rules.
