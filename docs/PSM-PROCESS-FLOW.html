PSM 1

Phase 1 — supervisor registration & defence preparation

The registration flow. Lampiran A is the entry point of the whole system.

1
Student submits Lampiran A with three candidate titles
student Lampiran A /registrations/new POST /api/registrations/agreements status → pending_supervisor
All three titles are now mandatory and must differ — the re-defence path needs alternatives to offer. Submitting with one title returns 422 "requires three candidate titles".
2
Supervisor acknowledges and picks the agreed title
supervisor Lampiran A, part C /registrations/:id POST …/acknowledge status → pending_jkpsm
Note: agreed_title is still free text — it is not yet constrained to one of the three candidates.
3
JKPSM / coordinator approves — the pairing is registered
coordinator Lampiran A, part D POST …/approve creates supervision_assignments
This is the step that creates the live student↔supervisor link, through AssignmentService so capacity rules still apply.
4
Coordinator fallback — auto-assign a supervisor after a timeframe
coordinator / systemno screen
Requirement: "If a student fails to secure a supervisor within the designated timeframe, the PSM Coordinator automatically assigns a supervisor." There is no timeframe setting, no scheduled job, and no auto-assignment. A coordinator can assign manually from /assignments.
5
Title Defence session in front of the two assigned examiners
coordinator starts panel records /title-defence POST /api/title-defences
The coordinator creates a sitting and starts it from their dashboard; it is open during the time and the panel can only record decisions while it is open. Closing it, or the window expiring, stops recording.
One collective decision per student — Approved, Conditional Approve or Rejected — with a mandatory reason for the latter two. The record mirrors the faculty's defence sheet column for column: proposed title, accepted title, decision, reason, project type and area, panel comments, and both panel names (taken from the examiner allocations).

Phase 2 — post-defence approval flow

The requirement branches three ways on the defence outcome. The decision is now recorded, but the branches it should trigger are not yet wired.

A
Approved → student submits Lampiran B → project created
student Lampiran B POST …/title-proposal project status → submitted
Lampiran B works and creates the project, but it is gated on Lampiran A being approved by JKPSM, not on the defence outcome — the decision is recorded but does not yet gate anything.
C
Conditional approve → status "Conditional Approval" → Lampiran C → Lampiran B
Lampiran Cno statusno form
Lampiran C is absent from the codebase entirely, and there is no "Conditional Approval" status to trigger it. Your form required/lampiranC.html documents what it needs: defence date, Examiner 1/2 names, previous vs new title, examiner comments → correction actions, JKPSM verification.
R
Rejected → student picks Title 2 or 3 → re-defence
no screen
The candidate set is now captured (three titles), but nothing reads it back to offer a re-defence. The data is in place; the flow is not.

Phase 3 — project execution & PSM 1 final evaluation

Fully implemented. Milestones now differ by PSM part (template v3).

1
Student submits deliverables against milestone deadlines
student /milestones POST /api/milestones/:id/submit
PSM 1 chain: proposal, chapter 1, chapter 2, chapter 3, chapter 4, final report (weights 10/15/15/15/20/25). Chapter 4 deliberately spans both parts — PSM 1 hands over the build, PSM 2 examines it in depth.
2
Supervisor evaluates with Lampiran E — only once all milestones are approved
supervisor Lampiran E /evaluations gated
The gate is new and applies to both allocation and submission. Trying it early returns 422 "Lampiran E … opens only once every milestone has been approved. N of M milestone(s) are still outstanding."
3
Two examiners grade with Lampiran I — system averages the two scores
examiner ×2 Lampiran I /assignments panel = 2
Final examiner score = (Examiner 1 + Examiner 2) ÷ 2 — the grouping averages all examiners. The panel is now capped at two; a third allocation returns 422 rather than silently changing the average.

PSM 2

Scope — chapters 4 to 7

Implementation, testing, results, conclusion.

1
Milestone chain
student chapter 4 → 7 + final report
Weights 20/20/15/15/30. Previously PSM 2 was given the same chain as PSM 1 (proposal + chapters 1–5); it now starts at chapter 4 as the requirement states.
2
Supervisor evaluates progress, implementation and final report
supervisor Lampiran G Lampiran H ×2 POST /api/evaluations/progress-report
G is the main evaluation; H is the progress report, taken twice (Laporan Kemajuan 1 and 2).
3
Two examiners grade the final presentation and thesis
examiner ×2 Lampiran J panel = 2
Same averaging rule as PSM 1.

Cross-cutting — what happens after marking

1
Grade aggregation
EvaluationService::computeFinalGrade() weighted per official form coordinator 0
Each official form carries its own share, and the forms marked are combined by weight: PSM 1 — Lampiran E 35 (supervisor) + Lampiran I 30 (examiners) = 65. PSM 2 — Lampiran G 50 + Lampiran H 5 (supervisor) + Lampiran J 40 (examiners) = 95.
Both totals sit below 100 because the remainder of the official weighting is marked outside this system, by the lecturer in class. What the system releases is therefore a mark over the components it holds — the weighted mean, rescaled so a part-marked project is not shown as failing — not a final grade. The full breakdown is persisted on the grade row, so any mark can be explained line by line.
The coordinator awards no marks at all, and since the weighting moved to forms they cannot adjust an assessor's mark either — that ability has been removed.
Weights live in psm.assessment_weights and are keyed by form, not by assessor role: G and H are both supervisor forms, so a per-role scheme would merge their 50 and 5.
2
Release → archive
/grades /archive /audit
Grades are released, the project is archived as a frozen snapshot, and every one of these actions lands in the append-only audit log.

Milestone state machine

Applies to every milestone in both parts. Illegal transitions are rejected by MilestoneService::transitionTo().

pending → open → submitted → reviewed → approved
submitted → rejected (revision requested, reopens for upload)
open/pending → overdue (deadline passed with no submission)
approved → unlocks the next milestone in the chain

Withdrawal rule (new): withdrawing a file from a submitted milestone returns it to rejected, even if other files remain — the reviewer must never assess a set that changed underneath them. Files are superseded, never deleted, so the audit trail keeps what was actually submitted.

Gap list — what to declare as not implemented

Everything here is Phase 2 (post-defence) plus one Phase 1 item. Nothing else in the document is missing.

Lampiran C — absent from the codebase. Required for the "Conditional Approve" branch, so a conditional decision currently has no way to be resolved.
Wiring the decision to the next step — the panel's decision is recorded and visible, but it does not yet gate Lampiran B. Gating it now would deadlock a conditional student, because Lampiran C does not exist to clear them.
"Conditional Approval" status — no such project or student status exists.
Re-defence from Title 2 or 3 — the three titles are captured, but nothing reads them back.
Coordinator auto-assign fallback — no timeframe, no scheduled job, no auto-assignment.

Also worth recording as a limitation

ItemDetail
Assessment weights Weighting totals PSM 1 weights total 65 and PSM 2 total 95, because the remainder of the official weighting is marked outside the system (lecturer, in class). The system therefore releases marks, not final grades. Milestones no longer contribute to the mark — they gate it instead, since Lampiran E cannot be completed until every milestone is approved.
Legacy chapter rubric A coordinator-level rubric still scores proposal + chapters 1–5 and has no chapter 6 or 7. Unreachable for the official forms (E/I/G/J), and coordinator weight is 0, but it is stale.
psm_part = BOTH No milestone template exists for it, so such a project could not be instantiated. The chains are now part-specific by design.
Lampiran K and R1 Both forms are in form required/ but are not in the requirement document's mapping matrix, so neither is implemented.
Leaderboard and public page Module 8 has been removed from the interface by decision — no leaderboard screens, no public showcase page, and no navigation entry. The API routes and tables still exist and are untouched, so it can be restored without rewriting anything. The requirement document's mapping matrix does not list a leaderboard, so nothing in the process flow above depends on it.
Archive downloads Seeded archive records show "not retrievable" — the demo files were never written to disk. Real archives carry a file reference and download normally.
